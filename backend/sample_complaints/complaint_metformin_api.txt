Complaint Description:
During incoming quality control testing of Metformin Hydrochloride API batch MFH260712A,
one drum out of 10 received drums showed assay results at 97.2% against the specification
of NLT 98.0% (as per IP monograph). The moisture content of the same drum was found to be
0.85% against the specification of NMT 0.5%. Other drums from the same batch tested within
specification. The affected drum was drum #7 (sequential labeling MFH260712A-D07).

Visual inspection of the affected drum showed no abnormalities in the physical appearance
of the API powder. No foreign particles, discoloration, or clumping was observed.

The complainant has quarantined the affected drum and requests investigation and replacement.
All remaining 9 drums are on hold pending resolution.

Supporting Documents Attached: COA, in-house testing report, drum photographs.
