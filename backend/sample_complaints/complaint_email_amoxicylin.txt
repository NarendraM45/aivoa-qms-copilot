Dear Quality Assurance Team,

I am writing to formally report a product quality complaint received from our dispensing counter at Apollo Pharmacy, MG Road Branch, Bangalore.

Product Details:
- Product Name: Amoxicylin Capsules IP
- Product Strength: 500 mg
- Batch/LOT Number: BMX24601
- Manufacturing Date: January 15, 2024
- Expiry Date: December 31, 2025
- Quantity Affected: 50 capsules (from 2 strips of 10x5)

Complaint Description:
During routine dispensing on July 25, 2026, our pharmacist noticed that approximately 50 capsules from the above batch displayed an abnormal yellowish-brown discoloration on the capsule shell. The discoloration was not uniform and appeared as irregular patches. The capsules were from sealed strips and had not been exposed to any unusual storage conditions. Our pharmacy maintains controlled storage at 25°C ± 2°C and 60% ± 5% RH as per label instructions.

No patients have consumed the affected capsules as our pharmacist identified the issue before dispensing. However, we have quarantined the remaining stock of batch BMX24601 at our facility (approximately 200 additional capsules across 4 strips).

We request an immediate investigation into the root cause and replacement of the affected stock.
